MOVEMENT PROPERTIES
☠ DESTROY
Eliminates any entity it collides with. If two DESTROY entities meet, both are annihilated. Cannot be consumed by DEVOUR.
◈ BOUNCE
Deflects off walls and other entities. Changes direction on every collision. Default movement behaviour.
◼ STOP
Freezes in place upon any collision. A second collision from another entity will unfreeze it, resuming movement.
◉ DEVOUR
Assimilates entities it collides with, absorbing their cells and growing larger. Cannot assimilate a DESTROY entity — instead gets eliminated by it.
⟳ CHANGE-[PROP]
Acts as the property shown in brackets upon collision, then immediately mutates to a different random property. The bracket label updates live to reflect the next behaviour. When two CHANGE-[DEVOUR] entities collide, the one created earlier wins — it absorbs the other and then mutates.

COLLISION REACTION
☠ DESTROY
Eliminates any entity it touches. If two DESTROY entities collide, both are annihilated simultaneously. DESTROY cannot be consumed by DEVOUR — it destroys the devourer instead.
◈ BOUNCE
Deflects off walls and other entities by picking a new random direction on each collision. Default collision behaviour. Two BOUNCE entities simply redirect each other.
◼ STOP
Freezes in place on first collision. When hit again by a moving entity, it resumes movement — launching in the same direction as the impact, like a physical push. The colliding entity bounces away as normal.
◉ DEVOUR
Absorbs any entity it touches, adding its cells to its own body and growing larger each time. Cannot consume DESTROY entities — contact with DESTROY eliminates it instead.
⟳ CHANGE-[PROP]
On collision, acts as the property shown in brackets, then immediately mutates into a new random property. The label updates live to show the next behaviour. Two CHANGE entities each apply their current bracket property simultaneously, then both mutate.

VISUAL REACTION
○ IGNORE
The entity takes no action based on its line of sight. It moves freely according to its collision rules only. Default behaviour.
→ FOLLOW
When the entity spots another entity in its line of sight, it locks on as a follow target and steers toward it every tick. The lock persists even after the target leaves direct sight — the entity keeps heading to the last known position. The lock is only released if the target is destroyed or the reaction is changed to IGNORE.
↩ AVOID
When the entity sees another entity whose Collision Reaction matches one of the selected Avoid Types, it steers away — moving in the same direction the threat is travelling, mirrored to flee. Avoidance is reactive: it only applies each tick that the threat is in direct line of sight. Use the Avoid Types selector to choose which collision types trigger this response; at least one type must remain selected.
